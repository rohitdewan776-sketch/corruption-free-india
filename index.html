Citizen Legal Rights & Anti-Corruption Laws

RTI Act 2005

Right to Information

Under the RTI Act, any citizen can request information from a public authority, which is required to reply within 30 days. This is a vital tool for exposing administrative delay and corruption.

Visit Official RTI Portal
Prevention of Corruption Act

PC Act, 1988

Demanding or accepting illegal gratification by public servants is a punishable crime. Giving a bribe under coercion is protected if reported within 7 days to enforcement authorities.
